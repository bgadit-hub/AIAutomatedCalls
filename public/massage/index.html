FAQ

Questions from massage studio owners

Can the AI match clients to the right therapist?+
Yes. We train the AI on each therapist’s specialty, style, and availability patterns. When a caller describes their needs, the AI recommends the right therapist and books directly with them. If there’s a preference conflict, it handles that gracefully too.
What booking systems does it connect to?+
We connect natively to Google Calendar, Mindbody, Jane App, Vagaro, GoHighLevel, and Calendly. Mindbody and Vagaro are the most common in massage studios — we connect to them during your setup session.
Can it handle membership questions and sign-ups?+
Yes. We train the AI on every membership tier, what’s included, pricing, and how to join. It walks callers through the options, answers every question, and routes them to your booking link or sign-up page to complete the membership.
How does it handle health intake questions?+
The AI is trained on your intake process — which conditions to flag, what to ask new clients, and when to recommend a different service. It sends your intake form automatically after booking so your therapist is prepared before the client arrives.
How quickly can we go live?+
Most massage studios go live within 48 hours. Knowledge extraction session, configuration, test calls, and deployment — you’re live before your next fully-booked Saturday.
