How quickly does it follow up on web leads?+
Within 3 minutes of a form submission, if you have outbound lead follow-up enabled. The AI calls the lead, introduces your program, qualifies them, and books the consultation — before they’ve had a chance to call a competitor.

Yes. We train the AI on your qualification criteria — BMI thresholds, medical history questions, contraindications, current medications. It captures this information during the intake call so your providers start every consultation with a prepared patient profile, not a blank intake form.

The AI handles scheduling, intake information collection, and general program questions — not clinical decision-making. Call recordings and transcripts are stored securely. We discuss your specific compliance requirements on the demo call and configure the AI accordingly.
