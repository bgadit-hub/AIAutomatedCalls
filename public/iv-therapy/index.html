FAQ

Questions from IV bar owners

Can the AI recommend specific drips to callers?+
Yes. We train the AI on your full drip menu, what each formula is designed for, and how your staff typically recommends treatments. It guides callers to the right drip based on their goal — recovery, energy, immunity, beauty — the way your best staff member would. It won’t give medical advice, but it handles treatment selection naturally.
What scheduling systems does it connect to?+
We connect natively to Google Calendar, GoHighLevel, Jane App, and Calendly. For other platforms, we discuss integration during your setup session. Most cloud-based booking systems can be connected.
Can it handle group and event bookings?+
Yes. We train the AI on your group booking process — minimum size, deposit requirements, how to capture the inquiry. For large groups it collects the details and routes to your team for confirmation, so nothing slips through.
How does it handle health screening questions?+
We train the AI on your intake screening process and basic contraindications. It handles the standard intake questions and routes anything that requires clinical judgment to your on-site staff. It never makes medical decisions.
How quickly can we go live?+
Most IV bars go live within 48 hours. Knowledge extraction session, configuration, test calls, and deployment — you’re live before the week is out.
