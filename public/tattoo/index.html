FAQ

Questions from tattoo studio owners

Can the AI handle design and style questions?+
Yes. We train the AI on your artists’ styles, the designs you specialize in, and how to explain the consultation process. It won’t critique reference images or make design decisions — that’s what the consultation is for — but it handles the initial inquiry and routes to the right artist.
What scheduling systems does it connect to?+
We connect natively to Google Calendar, GoHighLevel, Calendly, and Square Appointments. For other platforms, we discuss integration during setup. Most cloud-based booking systems can be connected.
Can it collect deposits automatically?+
Yes. After booking a consultation or session, the AI sends your deposit link via text automatically. The booking isn’t considered confirmed until the deposit is paid — reducing ghost bookings and no-shows.
How does it handle aftercare questions?+
We train the AI on your specific aftercare instructions — your wrapping method, washing routine, moisturizer recommendations, and what’s normal vs. what needs attention. It handles the routine questions and routes genuine healing concerns to you directly.
How quickly can we go live?+
Most tattoo studios go live within 48 hours. Knowledge extraction, configuration, test calls, deployment — you’re live before your next long session.
