Can the AI handle insurance questions accurately?+
Yes. During the knowledge extraction session, we train the AI on every insurance plan you accept, your typical out-of-pocket structure, and how you handle billing questions. It won’t verify specific benefits in real-time, but it will answer “do you take my plan?” accurately.
What scheduling systems does it connect to?+
We connect natively to Google Calendar, GoHighLevel, Jane App, and Calendly. For practice management systems like ChiroTouch and Genesis, we discuss integration options on the demo call.
Can it handle personal injury and auto accident calls?+
Yes. We train the AI on your PI intake process — what information you need, how your billing works with attorneys and insurance, and how to position the call for a PI patient vs. a self-pay patient.
Will new patients know they’re talking to an AI?+
Most won’t. Sub-300ms response, natural voice, trained to use your practice name and explain your services the way you do.
How long does setup take?+
Most chiropractic clients go live within 48 hours. We do a 90-minute knowledge extraction session, configure your AI, connect to your scheduling system, run test calls, and deploy.
