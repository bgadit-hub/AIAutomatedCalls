FAQ

Questions from PT clinic owners

Can the AI handle insurance and authorization questions?+
Yes. We train the AI on every plan you accept and your general authorization requirements. It answers “do you take my insurance?” accurately and routes specific authorization or benefits verification questions to your billing team. It won’t pull live insurance data, but it knows your plans.
What scheduling systems does it connect to?+
We connect natively to Google Calendar, GoHighLevel, Jane App, and Calendly. For PT-specific systems like WebPT, Clinicient, and Therabill, we discuss integration options on the demo call — it depends on your plan and API access. Most cloud-based systems can be connected.
Can it handle calls from referring physician offices?+
Yes. We train the AI on your top referring physicians and how to handle their office calls — referral receipt confirmation, scheduling the patient, and what information to capture. It treats physician office calls with the priority they deserve.
How does it handle workers’ comp or MVA cases?+
We train the AI on your workers’ comp and MVA intake process — what information you need, how billing works, and how to position the call. It captures claim number, employer, attorney info, and accident details before the first visit.
How quickly can we go live?+
Most PT clinics go live within 48 hours. We do a 90-minute knowledge extraction session, configure your AI, connect to your scheduling system, run test calls, and deploy. You review a test call before we go live.
