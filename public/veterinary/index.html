FAQ

Questions from veterinary clinic owners

How does the AI handle veterinary emergencies?+
The AI recognizes emergency language — trouble breathing, possible poisoning, seizure, hit by car — and immediately routes to your on-call vet or emergency partner clinic.
What scheduling systems does it connect to?+
We connect natively to Google Calendar, GoHighLevel, and Calendly. For cloud-based vet PMS systems like EzyVet, Shepherd, and Digitail, we build the connection during your setup session.
Can the AI handle prescription refill requests?+
Yes. Trained on your refill policy — which medications need a recent exam, which can be approved remotely.
Will pet owners know they're talking to an AI?+
Most won't. Sub-300ms response, warm natural voice, trained to use your clinic's name and compassionate language.
What happens during surgery or high-volume hours?+
The AI answers every call simultaneously — no queue, no hold music, no voicemail. Unlimited concurrent calls.
