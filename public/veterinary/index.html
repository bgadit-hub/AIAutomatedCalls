How does the AI handle veterinary emergencies?+
The AI recognizes emergency language — trouble breathing, possible poisoning, seizure, hit by car — and immediately routes to your on-call vet, your emergency partner clinic, or ASPCA Poison Control. It never puts an emergency caller through a standard booking flow.

Can the AI handle prescription refill requests?+
Yes. The AI is trained on your refill policy during onboarding — which medications require a recent exam, which can be approved remotely. It captures the pet name, medication, and owner details, then routes the request to your team for approval via text or your CRM.
Will pet owners know they’re talking to an AI?+
Most won’t. Sub-300ms response time with a warm, natural voice. We train the AI to use your clinic’s name and compassionate language specific to your practice. You can choose to disclose it’s an AI if you prefer full transparency — some clinic owners do, some don’t. We support either approach.
What happens during surgery or high-volume hours?+
The AI answers every call simultaneously — no queue, no hold music, no voicemail. Whether your whole team is in surgery or it’s a Monday morning rush, every caller gets an immediate response. There’s no limit on concurrent calls.
