FAQ

Questions from optometrists

Can the AI handle vision insurance questions accurately?+
Yes. We train the AI on every vision plan you accept — VSP, EyeMed, Davis Vision, Spectera — and your specific frame allowance, exam copay, and contact lens benefit structure. It answers benefit questions the way your best front desk person would.
What scheduling systems does it connect to?+
We connect natively to Google Calendar, GoHighLevel, and Calendly. For optometry-specific systems like RevolutionEHR, Eyefinity, and MaximEyes, we discuss integration options on the demo call. Cloud-based systems with API access can typically be connected during onboarding.
Can it handle contact lens reorder requests?+
Yes. We train the AI on your reorder process — how to confirm prescription validity, which brands you carry, and how to route the request to your optical team. It handles the intake, you handle the fulfillment.
How does it handle urgent eye care calls?+
We train the AI to recognize urgent language — sudden vision loss, eye injury, floaters, flashes — and route immediately to your on-call provider or urgent care guidance. It never puts an urgent caller through a standard booking flow.
How quickly can we go live?+
Most optometry practices go live within 48 hours. We do a 90-minute knowledge extraction session, configure your AI, connect to your scheduling system, and deploy after you approve a test call.
