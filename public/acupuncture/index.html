FAQ

Questions from acupuncturists

Can the AI explain acupuncture to someone who’s never tried it?+
Yes. We train the AI on how you explain acupuncture to first-timers — your philosophy, what a session feels like, what conditions you treat, and how many sessions patients typically need. It answers questions the way you would, in language that builds trust rather than sounding clinical.
What scheduling systems does it connect to?+
We connect natively to Google Calendar, GoHighLevel, Jane App, and Calendly. Jane App is particularly common in acupuncture practices — we can connect to it during your setup session. If you use another platform, ask us on the demo call.
Can it handle acupuncture insurance questions accurately?+
Yes. We train the AI on every plan you accept, your typical coverage scenarios, visit limits, and how to answer “will my insurance cover this?” in a way that’s accurate without over-promising. It routes specific verification questions to your billing process.
Will patients know they’re talking to an AI?+
Most won’t. Sub-300ms response, warm natural voice. Acupuncture patients tend to be holistic-minded — we train the AI to reflect your practice’s tone, whether that’s calm and grounding or direct and informational. You can choose to disclose it’s an AI if you prefer.
How quickly can we go live?+
Most acupuncture practices go live within 48 hours. We do a 90-minute knowledge extraction session, configure your AI, connect to your scheduling system, and deploy after you approve a test call.
