The veterinary call problem

Your team can't leave a patient on the table to answer the phone.

In a vet clinic, the phone can never be the priority — but for pet owners in panic, it absolutely is. Every missed call is a lost client, a missed recheck, or a pet that needed help.

Surgery & exam hours
Vets and techs can't leave a procedure to answer the phone. That's when your missed call rate spikes — and urgent callers call your competitor.
After-hours emergencies

FAQ

Questions from veterinary clinic owners

How does the AI handle veterinary emergencies?+
The AI recognizes emergency language — trouble breathing, possible poisoning, seizure, hit by car. It immediately routes to your on-call vet, emergency partner clinic, or ASPCA Poison Control. It never puts an emergency caller through a standard booking flow.
Can the AI handle prescription refill requests?+
Yes. Trained on your refill policy — which medications require a recent exam, which can be approved remotely. It captures the pet name, medication, and owner details and routes to your team for approval.
What practice management software does it integrate with?+
We integrate with Avimark, Cornerstone, EzyVet, Digitail, Shepherd, and VetSuite. Appointments book directly into your schedule without double-entry. If you use another system, we'll build the integration — included in setup.
Will pet owners know they're talking to an AI?+
Most won't. Sub-300ms response, warm compassionate voice. We train the AI to use your clinic's name and familiar language — “We're so sorry to hear Buddy isn't feeling well.” You can disclose it's an AI if you prefer full transparency.
What happens during surgery or high-volume hours?+
The AI answers every call simultaneously — no queue, no hold music, no voicemail. Whether your whole team is in surgery or it's Monday morning rush, every caller gets an immediate response. Unlimited concurrent calls.
