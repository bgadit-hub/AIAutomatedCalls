FAQ

Questions we get asked every week

How is AI Automated Calls different from a basic answering service?+
Unlike basic answering services that take messages, our AI is trained on your specific business — your services, pricing, FAQs, booking system, and objection responses. It books appointments directly, qualifies leads, and follows up automatically. It’s the difference between a message-taker and a trained employee.
How long does it take to get set up?+
Most clients go live within 48 hours. We conduct a knowledge extraction session, configure your AI, run test calls, and deploy. You review a test call before we go live.
Does the AI sound robotic?+
No. Sub-300ms response time, natural voice. Callers consistently can’t tell it’s an AI. You can listen to a demo call before we go live.
What happens when the AI can’t answer a question?+
The AI handles 80–90% of calls without escalation. For edge cases, it smoothly transfers to your team with a real-time summary of the conversation so far.
What booking systems do you integrate with?+
Google Calendar, GoHighLevel, Calendly, Jane App, and most platforms with API access. If you use something else, we discuss integration on the demo call — if it has an API, we can connect it.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes. No long-term contracts. Cancel with 30 days notice. Most clients stay because the ROI is clear — typically 8–15x return within 90 days.
