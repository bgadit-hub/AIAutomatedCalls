How is AI Automated Calls different from a basic answering service?+
Unlike basic answering services that take messages, our AI is trained on your specific business — your services, pricing, FAQs, booking system, and objection responses. It books appointments directly into your calendar, qualifies leads, and follows up automatically.
How long does it take to get set up?+
Most clients go live within 48 hours. We conduct a knowledge extraction session, configure your AI, run test calls, and deploy. You're live before the week is out.
Does the AI sound robotic?+
No. Sub-300ms response time, natural voice. Callers consistently can't tell it's an AI. You can listen to a demo call before we go live.
What happens when the AI can't answer a question?+
The AI handles 80–90% of calls without escalation. For edge cases, it smoothly transfers to your team with a real-time summary of what was said.

Google Calendar, GoHighLevel, Calendly, Mindbody, Jane App, Dentrix, and most scheduling systems via API. If you use something else, we'll build the integration — included in setup.
