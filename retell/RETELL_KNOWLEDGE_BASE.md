## NICHE-SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE

### Dental Practices
- Average new patient value: $350 first visit; $2,400 lifetime
- Common pain: after-hours calls, appointment no-shows (20–30% industry average), missed calls during procedures
- Key selling point: AI reduces no-shows by sending automated reminders, reducing that 25% to under 10%
- Compliance note: We are not HIPAA-covered for the AI calls themselves (we handle scheduling, not medical information). For HIPAA-sensitive topics, AI routes to voicemail or says "please call during business hours for medical questions."
- Popular calendar integrations: Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Curve, Open Dental (via API or Google Cal bridge)

### Real Estate Agents
- Average transaction value: $8,000–15,000 commission
- Common pain: leads go cold in 5 minutes if not called back; agents miss calls during showings
- Key selling point: AI calls web leads within 60 seconds of form submission — 9× more likely to connect vs calling 30 minutes later (study: Lead Response Management)
- Pain stat: 78% of deals go to the first agent who responds (NAR, 2024)

### HVAC / Plumbing / Home Services
- Average job value: $350–800 service call; $5,000–15,000 installs
- Common pain: emergency calls after hours, dispatcher bottleneck, lost calls during busy season
- Key selling point: After-hours emergency call handling — AI captures the job, sends tech details, confirms appointment
- Key differentiator: 24/7 coverage is table stakes in home services — every missed emergency call is a lost job

### Med Spas / Aesthetics
- Average visit value: $400–800; lifetime value $3,000–8,000
- Common pain: front desk overwhelmed, leads from Instagram/ads go cold overnight
- Key selling point: Instant follow-up on all social/web leads; appointment booking without phone tag

### Law Firms
- Average case value: $3,000–15,000+
- Common pain: clients call multiple firms simultaneously; first to respond wins the case
- Key selling point: AI answers in under 2 rings, qualifies the case type, books a consultation
- Compliance note: AI does not provide legal advice. It qualifies (practice area match, state, urgency) and books consult only.

### Chiropractic / Physical Therapy
- Average new patient value: $180 first visit; $1,200 care plan
- Common pain: front desk is one person, calls during treatments go unanswered
- Key selling point: New patient intake automation — AI captures symptoms, insurance, preferred times before they ever walk in

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: How long does setup take?**
A: 48 hours from payment. We build your agent, configure it to your specific practice, test it with 50+ calls, then hand it over.

**Q: What calendar systems does it integrate with?**
A: Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly, Acuity, Jane App, Mindbody, Vagaro, and most practice management systems via API or webhook. If you use something else, we'll check within 24 hours.

**Q: Can we customize what the AI says?**
A: Yes — we configure it completely for your business: your name, your hours, your services, your FAQs, your voice if you want. Feels like your own virtual staff member.

**Q: What happens when someone asks something it doesn't know?**
A: It says "I want to make sure I get you the right answer on that — let me have someone from the team follow up with you directly." Then it logs the question and we update the knowledge base.

**Q: Is there a contract?**
A: Month-to-month. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice. We're confident enough in the results that we don't need to lock you in.

**Q: What if call volume is higher than expected?**
A: The AI scales with no additional cost — it handles unlimited concurrent calls. Your monthly fee stays the same regardless of volume.

**Q: Do you offer a refund?**
A: Yes — 14-day satisfaction guarantee on the setup fee. If you're not happy after 14 days and at least 50 calls, we refund the setup fee in full.

**Q: How do I see what the AI is doing?**
A: You get a dashboard showing every call — recording, transcript, outcome, appointments booked. Plus a monthly report emailed to you automatically.

**Q: What if someone is rude or has an emergency?**
A: The AI is programmed to de-escalate politely and can transfer to your on-call number for genuine emergencies. For rude callers, it remains calm and professional regardless.
